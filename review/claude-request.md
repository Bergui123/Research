# Independent manuscript review request

Please act as a skeptical mathematical and quantum algorithms referee. Review the complete manuscript `paper/Paper.tex`, its included TeX files, the long and short research plans, the relevant simulation scripts and Lean sources, and the project instructions. Look for internal contradictions, unjustified assumptions, incorrect derivations, misleading resource estimates or confidence claims, missing circuit costs, discrepancies between the source, code, figures and Lean formalization, and barriers to arXiv submission. Cross-check equations and actual implementations; do not treat agreement between documents or Lean theorem names as proof. Prioritize concrete, verifiable findings with file names and line numbers, severity, explanation, and a precise corrective action. Identify things you could not verify. Do not edit files or execute potentially destructive commands. Output your review in Markdown so it can be saved and discussed.
